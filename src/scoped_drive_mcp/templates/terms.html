Terms of service

These are the terms linked from the Google sign-in screen for Scoped Drive.

Scoped Drive is a gateway that sits in front of Google Drive.

Drive’s API does not give an app a fine-grained way to share only certain files and folders. An app that signs in is handed a broad Drive permission: the whole Drive of the account that approved it, or only files that app itself created. There is no Drive setting for “this app may use these folders through the API, and nothing else.”

This server is that control. You sign in, tick the files and folders an assistant may use, and every API call is checked against that list before it reaches Drive. Anything else is refused. If the assistant asks for something you have not allowed, you get an approval link and can allow or deny it. You can untick an item at any time.

Only the Google account configured as the owner of this deployment can sign in to the dashboard. An assistant reaches Drive only with a connection that owner created, and only for the items that owner allowed.

Using it

Signing in, or connecting an assistant, means you are the owner of this deployment or you are using it with that owner’s permission. Use it for your own Drive. Do not use it to reach files that are not yours.

The assistant you connect receives the contents of files you allowed. That is what the gateway is for. What the assistant does with those contents is covered by the assistant’s own terms.

You can untick items in the dashboard, disconnect the assistant, and remove this app’s access from your Google Account. Doing that stops further use. It does not recall anything an assistant has already received.

The service is a personal tool. It can be unavailable when its host, its database, or Google is unavailable. Your files stay in Google Drive. This server does not keep a copy of them.

Scoped Drive is not made by Google, and these terms are not Google’s terms. Privacy describes what is stored.
