Scoped Drive

Scoped Drive is a gateway that sits in front of Google Drive.

You sign in with your own Google account, then tick the files and folders an assistant may use. Each person’s files stay separate.

Drive’s API cannot limit an app to those items, so this server checks every call against your list before it reaches Drive. Anything you have not allowed is refused. If the assistant asks for something else, you get a link to allow or deny it.

The assistant uses the Google account you sign in with. The list you tick is what limits it. Google does not hide the rest of that Drive from this sign-in.
