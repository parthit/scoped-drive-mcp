How to pair
  1. Ask your assistant to start a pairing. It reads you a short code.
  2. Give that code to the other person. Their assistant joins with it.{% if self_pairing %} To pair two of your own assistants, say Claude and Cursor, join from your other assistant.{% endif %}
  3. Both of you approve here, after you see the other Google account.

A pairing with another person lets each of you read (only read) what the other shares. Nothing else in your Drive is reachable, and your assistants never message each other: another person’s assistant can’t send anything to yours.{% if self_pairing %} Only two of your own assistants can message each other.{% endif %}
